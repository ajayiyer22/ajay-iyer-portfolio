Overview

A CAD-guided robotic welding platform that connects workpiece geometry to torch motion and welder commands. The documented implementation covers registration, planning and execution before the later closed-loop process-control extension.

  1. Register the part
  2. Sequence seams
  3. Construct torch poses
  4. Plan & time motion
  5. Execute & inspect

Technical contributions

  • Registration and geometry. Developed a workflow for pairing CAD features with measured workpiece points and registering them to the robot frame. Selected seam geometry supplies welding targets, torch-orientation hints and approach/retreat waypoints.
  • Motion generation. Integrated pose interpolation, MoveIt planning and trajectory timing based on the requested travel speed. Joint-motion diagnostics and reconstructed tool positions support inspection of the planned motion.
  • Operator interface and execution. Combined PyQt5 and RViz with robot execution and welder voltage, wire-feed and trigger commands. The interface brings geometry selection, motion visualization and coordinated hardware operation into one workflow.

Results

Integrated CAD-to-robot execution with trajectory diagnostics and tool-position logging. Later work added infrared monitoring and process feedback for travel-speed and arc-voltage regulation.
