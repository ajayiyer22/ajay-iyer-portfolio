Needle-tip tracking with Kalman filtering

Ultrasound needle tracking using robust image processing and Kalman filtering.

Overview

An ultrasound needle-tracking pipeline combining robust image processing with temporal state estimation. Developed in LabVIEW, the system estimates needle-tip position under noisy image conditions and compares performance against an independent electromagnetic reference.

  1. Ultrasound image
  2. Extract needle features
  3. RANSAC measurement
  4. Kalman estimate

Technical contributions

  • Needle-feature extraction. Built background-subtraction and edge-detection stages to isolate candidate needle features within a region of interest. Applied Random Sample Consensus line fitting to reduce the influence of speckle and unrelated image edges.
  • Temporal estimation. Integrated a four-state Kalman filter to combine predicted state with image measurements across frames. This reduces reliance on individual noisy observations when estimating needle-tip position.
  • Experimental validation. Evaluated tracking in artificial and biological tissue at insertion depths up to 6.5 cm. Used an NDI-Aurora electromagnetic reference to compare the combined estimator against vision-only tracking.

Results

Position RMSE fell from 0.79 to 0.03 cm in artificial tissue and from 0.84 to 0.21 cm in biological tissue versus vision-only tracking, under the reported experimental conditions.
