Needle-tip tracking with Kalman filtering

Ultrasound needle-tip tracking that combines robust geometric fitting with recursive state estimation to reduce the effect of speckle and unreliable image features.

Research focus

I investigated how temporal estimation can improve needle tracking when ultrasound images provide uncertain measurements. The approach combines image-derived needle geometry with a Kalman filter to update the tip estimate across frames.

Methods and contributions

  • Robust feature extraction. Built a LabVIEW pipeline with background subtraction and edge detection. Applied Random Sample Consensus (RANSAC) line fitting within a region of interest to estimate needle geometry despite outlying image features.
  • Recursive estimation. Used a four-state Kalman Filter (KF) to combine predicted state with image measurements, maintaining a temporal estimate when speckle obscured the needle tip.
  • Experimental comparison. Compared tracking against an NDI-Aurora electromagnetic reference in artificial and biological tissue, at insertion depths up to 6.5 cm.

Results

Relative to vision-only tracking, position root mean square error (RMSE) decreased from 0.79 to 0.03 cm in artificial tissue and from 0.84 to 0.21 cm in biological tissue.
