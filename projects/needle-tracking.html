Needle-tip tracking with Kalman filtering

Combined ultrasound image processing and Kalman filtering to track a needle tip against electromagnetic reference measurements.

Problem & goal

Ultrasound speckle and unrelated edges can make needle tip localization unstable from frame to frame. I built a LabVIEW tracking pipeline and compared its position estimates with an independent electromagnetic reference.

  1. Ultrasound image
  2. Extract needle features
  3. RANSAC measurement
  4. Kalman estimate

Approach & implementation

  • Image measurements. Used background subtraction and edge detection in a region of interest, then fit a needle line with RANSAC to reject unrelated image edges.
  • State estimation. Used a four state Kalman filter to combine predicted motion with image measurements across frames.
  • Validation. Tested insertion in artificial and biological tissue to depths of 6.5 cm, comparing the combined estimator with vision only tracking using an NDI Aurora reference.

Implementation & results

Position RMSE decreased from 0.79 to 0.03 cm in artificial tissue and from 0.84 to 0.21 cm in biological tissue compared with vision only tracking under the reported experimental conditions.
