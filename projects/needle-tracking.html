Needle-tip tracking with Kalman filtering

Combining ultrasound image processing and a four-state Kalman filter to estimate needle-tip position when speckle obscures the visual measurement.

The problem

Ultrasound speckle can make a needle tip difficult to detect consistently. I combined geometric image processing with state estimation to maintain a position estimate through uncertain visual observations.

Engineering work

Visual measurement

Built a LabVIEW pipeline using background subtraction, edge detection, and region-of-interest RANSAC line fitting to extract needle features.

State estimation

Used a four-state Kalman filter to combine measurements over time and maintain needle-tip position estimates when speckle obscured the tip.

Experimental comparison

Compared the estimates against an NDI-Aurora electromagnetic tracker in artificial and biological tissue, with insertion depths up to 6.5 cm.

Results & scope

Compared with vision-only tracking, position root mean square error (RMSE) decreased from 0.79 cm to 0.03 cm in artificial tissue and from 0.84 cm to 0.21 cm in biological tissue. These results apply to the artificial- and biological-tissue experimental setup.
