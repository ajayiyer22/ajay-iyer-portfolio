Overview

An internship project investigating joint robot-pose and landmark estimation through EKF-SLAM simulation. A separate embedded implementation explores obstacle avoidance and encoder-based motion feedback on a SPARK-V robot.

Technical contributions

  • Probabilistic estimation. Adapted a MATLAB EKF-SLAM simulator to estimate robot pose and landmark positions together. Implemented prediction and measurement updates with Jacobian-based uncertainty propagation.
  • Landmark association. Applied uncertainty-aware nearest-neighbor matching with Mahalanobis gating to associate range–bearing measurements with landmarks. Examined how revisiting landmarks changes uncertainty in the estimated map.
  • Embedded navigation. Extended simulated reactive steering and separately programmed ultrasonic obstacle avoidance and encoder feedback in Embedded C. The SPARK-V implementation used its ATmega16 controller for room-traversal experiments.

Results

Repeated landmark observations reduced map uncertainty in simulation. The SPARK-V robot separately demonstrated room traversal with obstacle avoidance.
