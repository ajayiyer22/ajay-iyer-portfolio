EKF-SLAM and embedded robot navigation

A study of joint robot-pose and landmark estimation in MATLAB, alongside ultrasonic obstacle avoidance and encoder-based navigation on a SPARK-V robot.

The problem

A mobile robot must reason about its pose while its sensors and motion are noisy. This project combined a simulation study of probabilistic mapping with a separate physical implementation of obstacle-avoiding navigation.

Engineering work

SLAM simulation

Adapted a MATLAB EKF-SLAM simulator for joint pose and landmark estimation. Studied Jacobian linearization and nearest-neighbor data association with Mahalanobis gating.

Mapping behavior

Observed reduced landmark uncertainty over repeated waypoint traversals. Extended the simulator with reactive steering using three sensor views to navigate around landmarks without preset waypoints.

Embedded navigation

Implemented navigation on a SPARK-V robot in Embedded C for its ATmega16 controller. Combined ultrasonic obstacle avoidance with wheel-encoder distance feedback and demonstrated traversal of a room containing obstacles.

Results & scope

The work connected the mathematics of estimation to practical mobile-robot sensing and control. EKF-SLAM was studied in MATLAB; the SPARK-V demonstration implemented obstacle avoidance and encoder feedback rather than a full onboard SLAM stack.
