A robot swarm searching for light

Cooperative light-source search with three mobile robots, using Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) to turn individual measurements into a shared search strategy.

Research focus

I investigated how local measurements can guide collective search when the brightest location is unknown. The project translated a population-based optimization method into coordinated movement on three LEGO NXT robots.

Methods and contributions

  • Collective search. Represented each robot as a PSO particle, combining its personal-best light observation with the global best found by the swarm. Developed the search in MATLAB and implemented the hardware workflow in Python.
  • Visual localization. Tracked the robots with an overhead camera and ArUco fiducial markers. Supplied position feedback to a centralized controller communicating with the robots over Bluetooth.
  • Navigation and sensing. Integrated light measurements, waypoint following, and ultrasonic obstacle avoidance so the robots could pursue search locations around static obstacles and one another.

Results

The robots converged on the brightest region in the arena; one documented trial required 11 iterations. The related paper received a Best Paper Award at ICECA 2019.
