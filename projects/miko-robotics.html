Engineering focus

At MIKO, I worked on the connection between robot dynamics and interactive behavior. My role combined system identification, controller synthesis, sensor integration, and hardware experiments across several motion-control problems.

Methods and contributions

  • System identification and articulation. Used measured mechanism responses to inform dynamic models and robust controller design for a head-motion manipulator. Developed path-planning and control software for expressive joint motion.
  • Balance control. Integrated the sensor suite of a self-balancing robot and tuned feedback controllers to regulate balance. Evaluated stability and motion on the physical platform.
  • Perception-driven orientation. Mapped audio direction-of-arrival (DoA) estimates to yaw-axis control, enabling the robot to face an active speaker. Added remote-control functionality for the mobile platform.

Engineering contribution

This work carried control methods through modeling, implementation, and hardware testing, connecting stability and tracking requirements to expressive robot behavior.
