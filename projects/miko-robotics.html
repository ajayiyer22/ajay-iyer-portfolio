The problem

An interactive robot must turn sensing into motion that is stable, repeatable, and expressive. My work connected dynamic modeling and controller design to joint motion, balance, and directional responses on robot hardware.

Engineering work

Head-motion manipulator

Developed control and path-planning software for expressive joint-level articulation. Used system identification, stability testing, and robust controller synthesis to connect a model of the mechanism to its physical response.

Self-balancing mobility

Extended the control work to a self-balancing robot, integrating its sensor suite and tuning robust controllers to maintain balance.

Audio-directed motion

Connected direction-of-arrival estimates from audio processing to yaw-axis control so the robot could turn toward an active speaker. Also implemented remote-control functionality for the mobile platform.

  1. Identify dynamics
  2. Design controller
  3. Integrate sensors
  4. Test on hardware

Results & scope

This role gave me experience taking control algorithms through modeling, implementation, sensor integration, and hardware testing. It continues to shape how I approach equipment automation: understand the dynamics, make the feedback path explicit, and evaluate the behavior on the system.
