Ajay Ganesan Iyer

I’m a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at Texas A&M University. My research focuses on multi-robot exploration and search. I study how robots with different mobility and sensing capabilities can use a shared map to decide which areas each robot should explore.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as a robotics engineer at MIKO, developing motion control for consumer robot prototypes. At Texas A&M, I’ve also worked on perception and planning for autonomous vehicles on rural roads and robotic welding.
