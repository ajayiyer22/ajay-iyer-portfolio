01 / About

Connecting models
to machines.

I’m a Mechanical Engineering Ph.D. student at Texas A&M University, working across robotics, motion planning, and control.

My current research with Dr. Srikanth Saripalli explores how semantic information can guide robots toward useful inspection viewpoints. Previously, I worked on rural-road perception and planning with Dr. Reza Langari and robotic welding automation with Dr. Prabhakar Pagilla.

Before my Ph.D., I spent two years as a Robotics Engineer at MIKO. Building and testing physical robots taught me to consider the whole system: dynamics, sensors, software, and the behavior that emerges when they run together.

My academic foundation is in Systems and Control at IIT Roorkee and Instrumentation and Control at NIT Trichy. I’m interested in engineering roles where I can turn that foundation into useful robot behavior.
