01 / About

Connecting models
to machines.

I’m a Mechanical Engineering Ph.D. student at Texas A&M University, working across robotics, motion planning, and control.

With Dr. Srikanth Saripalli, I investigate how robot teams divide exploration and search tasks as they build a shared map. My current work combines sensor fusion, frontier-based allocation, and visual detection; ongoing research examines capability-aware coordination and semantic viewpoint selection.

Previously, I studied rural-road perception and trajectory planning with Dr. Reza Langari, and CAD-guided welding with Dr. Prabhakar Pagilla. Before my Ph.D., I spent two years at MIKO translating dynamic models and sensor feedback into expressive motion and self-balancing control.

I hold a master’s degree in Systems and Control from IIT Roorkee and a bachelor’s degree in Instrumentation and Control from NIT Trichy.
